## Overview

Pabs brings food delivery, grocery runs, and general "Pasugo" errands into a single
mobile experience. Customers, merchants, and riders each get a tailored interface
backed by a shared real-time dispatch system.

Visit [pabs.online](https://pabs.online) for the live product.
